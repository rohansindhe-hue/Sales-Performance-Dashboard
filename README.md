# Sales-Performance-Dashboard

The Dashboard is a one-page sales view for 2026, built from 2,000 orders. It has KPI tiles and five charts. Four slicers (Region, Sales Channel, Customer Type, Product Category) filter everything at once.

Headline KPIs

Total revenue: about $3.46M
Units sold: 7,552
Orders: 2,000
Average revenue per order: about $1,731

What the charts show

Revenue by month: Revenue is uneven. The peaks are Jun ($375K), Nov ($371K), Apr ($348K) and Mar ($323K). The lows are Jul ($215K), May ($228K) and Jan ($235K).
Revenue by region: Central leads at about $803K, followed by North ($787K) and East ($658K). South ($628K) and West ($587K) are the weakest.
Revenue by product category: Computers dominate at about $2.06M, roughly 59% of revenue. Monitors follow at $737K, then Networking ($292K) and Storage ($270K). Accessories and Office Supplies together add little (about $105K).
Revenue by sales channel: Online is the biggest at $1.11M, then Corporate Sales ($1.01M) and Retail Store ($907K). Marketplace is smallest at $429K.
Top 5 products: Gaming Laptop is the clear leader at about $666K. Laptop Pro 14 ($404K), Laptop Air 13 ($403K), All-in-One PC ($390K) and Ultrawide Monitor ($272K) follow. These five together make up about $2.13M, or around 62% of total revenue.

Takeaways

Revenue depends heavily on computers, especially laptops.
Online and Corporate Sales together bring in about 61% of revenue.
Regional spread is fairly even, though Central is ahead.
Monthly revenue swings by almost 75% between the best and worst months, so seasonality or a few large orders may be driving it.
